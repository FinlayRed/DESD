Why Bristol Regional Food Network?

🥬 Fresh & Local

All products come from producers within 20 miles of Bristol, ensuring freshness and supporting the local economy.

🌱 Know Your Food

Full traceability from farm to table. See exactly where your food comes from and how it's produced.

🤝 Fair for Producers

Only 5% network commission means producers keep 95% of the sale price. Weekly settlements ensure reliable income.

♻️ Sustainable

Reduced food miles, seasonal eating, and direct relationships between customers and producers.

How It Works

  1. Browse — Explore products from local farms, dairies, bakeries, and more
  2. Order — Add items to your basket (48-hour lead time for fresh preparation)
  3. Pay — Secure payment, with 95% going directly to producers
  4. Collect — Pick up from the producer or arrange local delivery
